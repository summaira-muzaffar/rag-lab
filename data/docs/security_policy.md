# Security Policy (Acme Corp)

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company accounts.

Suspected phishing emails must be reported to security@acme.example within 1 hour. A lost or stolen laptop must be reported to IT within 2 hours.

Personal USB drives must not be connected to company devices. Company data is classified in four levels: public, internal, confidential and restricted. Restricted data may only be shared with explicit written approval.
