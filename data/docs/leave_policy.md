# Leave Policy (Acme Corp)

Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year; anything beyond that expires on 31 March.

Sick leave is 10 days per year. A doctor's note is required if the sick leave lasts more than 3 consecutive days.

Parental leave is 16 weeks of fully paid leave for either parent. Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance, except for sick leave and emergencies.
