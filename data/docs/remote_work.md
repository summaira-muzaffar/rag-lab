# Remote Work Policy (Acme Corp)

Acme follows a hybrid model. Employees work from the office three days a week: Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Monday and Friday are remote days.

Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, and everyone must be reachable during those hours.

Employees receive a remote work allowance of $40 per month to cover home internet. A company VPN must be used whenever connecting to internal systems from outside the office. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years.
